5. TEACHING CONTENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Teaching cards, the search lexicon, the spotter bank and the competency map in
app/data/anatomy.json are original content written for this product by
MASK360 Agency. They are not part of the BodyParts3D dataset and are not
covered by its licence.

Content is written to the standard of Gray's Anatomy for Students, Cunningham's
Manual of Practical Anatomy, BD Chaurasia's Human Anatomy, Snell's Clinical
Anatomy and Last's Anatomy, using Terminologia Anatomica naming. No text is
reproduced from those works.

6. INTENDED USE AND LIMITATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Anatomy Studio is an EDUCATIONAL PRODUCT. It is not a medical device. It models
one scanned adult male. It must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, surgical
planning, or the measurement of any real person. The Coverage panel inside the
application states in full what the model contains and what it does not.
