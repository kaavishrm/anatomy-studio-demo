WHAT THIS MEANS COMMERCIALLY

The mesh data cannot be locked down. Anyone who receives this build may lawfully
redistribute the geometry files under CC BY-SA. Any commercial agreement must
therefore be priced and written against what actually IS exclusive and
enforceable:

  · the software layer and its interface
  · the teaching content, the spotter bank and the competency mapping
  · hosting, LAN deployment and updates
  · institutional branding
  · faculty training
  · support and the annual content refresh

Do not sell "per-seat access to the model". Sell an annual institutional licence
to the software, the content and the service around them.

Have this reviewed by counsel before signing an institutional agreement.
