The 119 codes, and where we are probably wrong

Anatomy Studio maps 119 NMC CBME anatomy competencies to the structures each one is demonstrated on. The codes and their wording were reconstructed, not quoted from the NMC document. We are certain some of them are wrong.

This page exists so an anatomy department can find the wrong ones. Read down the list, mark what does not match your copy of the curriculum, and press Copy corrections at the bottom. Paste the result into an email. We fix them and name the department that caught them.
